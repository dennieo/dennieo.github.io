To deliver a page, GitHub’s servers receive your IP address and standard request details such as the page requested, the time and your browser. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. I do not receive these logs. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in running a secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Analytics, only with your consent

If you accept, the site uses Google Analytics 4 (provided by Google Ireland Limited for visitors in the EEA and the UK, and by Google LLC elsewhere) to understand which pages are read and which links are used. It records the pages you view, the site that referred you, your device and browser type, your approximate location (country or city, derived from your IP address, which Google Analytics 4 does not store), clicks on outbound links such as the App Store or LinkedIn, the fact that an email link was used (not the address), and when the résumé is printed or saved as a PDF.

Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off, so the data is not used for advertising and is not linked to your Google account. Event data is kept for 2 months. Google processes this data on my behalf under its data processing terms.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive). You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page; withdrawing consent stops Google Analytics and deletes its cookies.

Cookies and local storage

  • imdennie-consent (local storage, this site): remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics. Strictly necessary; kept for about 6 months, after which the banner asks again.
  • _ga (cookie, Google Analytics, only after you accept): distinguishes visitors. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_SXF5SS0QYH (cookie, Google Analytics, only after you accept): keeps the state of your visit. Expires after 2 years.

There are no advertising cookies, social media pixels or other trackers.

Fonts and links

The DM Sans typeface is served from this site, not from Google Fonts. Links to other services (for example LinkedIn, the App Store or project websites) take you to sites that have their own privacy policies.

Email

If you write to me, I use your email address and message to reply and to discuss any project you raise. My email is hosted by Google (Gmail). Your rights
